Crawl Space Water Removal Help in Davis

A flooded crawl space rarely announces itself. Most people find it when the floors feel cold and damp, a musty smell comes up through the vents, or someone opens the access hatch and sees a pond. At that point the batts are typically saturated and the framing overhead has sat damp for several days. Our team pumps the water out, clears what is ruined and dries the framing before it softens.

Once the standing water is gone, the job is only halfway done. Wood framing takes water in gradually and gives it up just as gradually, which is why we meter the joists, beams and subfloor and leave dehumidifiers on until readings drop, typically 3 to 5 days. Finding the cause matters just as much, since a dripping water line, a failed drain or groundwater from a storm will flood the area again unless it is addressed.

Warning Signs

  • Fiberglass insulation sagging or hanging down between the floor joists
  • Sweating pipes and ductwork that drip onto the ground beneath the home
  • A first floor with a cool, moist feel and some give when you walk on it
  • Steel posts, hangers for pipes and duct straps showing orange rust
  • A musty, earthy smell that is strongest near the floor vents
  • Fuzzy patches, pale or dark, spreading across the framing or subfloor
Our Process

Our Crawl Space Water Removal Process

1

Safety Check and Inspection

Before a technician goes under the house, we look for live electrical dangers, fallen ductwork and dirty water. Then we map how far the water reached and take moisture readings on the joists and subfloor.

2

Remove Standing Water

Submersible pumps and extraction hoses remove the standing water, including the low spots and footing trenches where it collects. While pumping, the crew also cleans off the sediment the water left on the plastic.

3

Remove Wet Materials

We bag up the wet insulation, damaged vapor barrier and loose debris and take it all outside. Where mold has begun or is likely, the bare joists and subfloor get cleaned and an antimicrobial applied.

4

Dry and Verify

Low profile air movers and a dehumidifier run under the house until the wood reaches a normal moisture reading. We recheck with a meter and give you the readings and a prevention plan.

What You Cannot See

What Hides Behind It

Weakened Floor Framing

Decay tends to begin along the upper edge of the framing or where it meets the sill, out of view from the access opening. We probe the wood to find soft spots.

Contaminated Water

Do not assume the water below your floor is clean. If it came from a drain pipe or from waste laden groundwater it is rated Category 2 or 3, so we suit up and disinfect.

Shifting Piers and Supports

Long periods of standing water loosen the soil that holds up the support piers. You might notice it as a door that suddenly rubs or a floor that is no longer level.

Air From Below

A good share of the air on your first floor rises from the crawl space, so mold spores and damp odors down there end up in the rooms you live in.

The Damage Timeline

Day one

Puddles Form Underneath

The dirt floor starts absorbing moisture as puddles form in every dip. Insulation starts wicking it up and humidity under the floor climbs fast.

Hours 24 to 48

Early Mold Growth

Mold can start on damp joists and paper faced insulation. The weight of the water drags the batts loose from the underside of the subfloor.

Day 3 to 7

Moisture Moves Upstairs

Humid air from below works its way up into the rooms. The lumber goes on taking in moisture, a stale smell spreads and flooring can begin to cup.

After a week

Wood Starts Rotting

Decay begins in any framing that never got a chance to dry. Joists and sill plates soften, fasteners rust and the damp space attracts termites and other pests.
